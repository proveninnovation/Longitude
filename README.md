# Longitude — shared resources

Published pages for a **Cessna Citation Longitude (Model 700, CE-700, Garmin G5000)**
two-crew setup in **Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024** (Asobo airframe, Working Title
G5000 avionics).

> [!warning] Sim use only — not for real flight
> Nothing here is an AFM. The checklist items are transcribed from Section XVII of a
> community-written *Model 700 Operators Guide* that is itself stamped *"for use in
> Microsoft Flight Simulator only"*. The crew mechanics are a proposal, not an
> operator's SOP.

## What is here

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`index.html`](https://proveninnovation.github.io/Longitude/) | The **EFB** — interleaved flows and checklists, PF/PM role filter, G5000 entry detail, and a per-line confidence rating |

## The confidence chips

Every line on the EFB carries a six-cell chip saying **how well sourced it is** — not how
sure the wording sounds. Green means a real Longitude crew is on record doing it; red means
it is our own judgement and wants confirming. Hover a chip for the evidence behind it.

`Conf` in the header hides them. `Review` numbers every line, so a change can be named
precisely — *"reword 13.c"*, *"swap 13.2 and 13.6"*.
